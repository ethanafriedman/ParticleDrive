UNDER THE HOOD

What is happening?

  1. Predict where the car may have moved.
  2. Compare each particle with landmark readings.
  3. Resample likely positions more often.
  4. Estimate the car's position from the particles.

Try turning off sensors, then turning them back on. Watch the orange estimate recover while the red odometry path keeps drifting.

Built with Python, NumPy, FastAPI, and vanilla JavaScript. Localization is simulated, not a real driving system.
